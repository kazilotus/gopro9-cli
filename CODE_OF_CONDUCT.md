# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) v2.1.

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a
harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the
maintainer at kazilotus@hotmail.com. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly
and fairly.

For the full text, see https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/.
